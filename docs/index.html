NCERT Chapter 3 Key Concepts

Class 9 Science • Tissues in Action

1. Apical Meristem

Located at the growing tips of stems and roots. Responsible for linear elongation (increase in length).

2. Lateral Meristem (Cambium)

Causes the girth (thickness) of stems and roots to increase over time.

3. Intercalary Meristem

Located at internodes or the base of leaves. Allows rapid regrowth after mowing or grazing (e.g. grass).

4. Permanent Tissues

Once meristematic cells mature and take up specific roles, they lose the ability to divide (differentiation).
